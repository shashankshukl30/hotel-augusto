Getting here

## Between the airport and the river.

By air LBS International Airport is about 20 km up the Babatpur road. The travel desk arranges pickups; tell us your flight the day before.

By rail Varanasi Junction (Cantt) is about 2 km. Prepaid autos and cabs reach Shivpur in 15–20 minutes.

To the river Dashashwamedh Ghat is about 6 km. Leave early for first light on the water; we will book the boat.

*   Distances from the hotel

*   Kashi Vishwanath Temple 6 km
*   Dashashwamedh Ghat 6 km
*   Sarnath 5 km
