Hotel Augusto

D 36/1 Godowlia, Dasaswmedh Ghat Jangambari Road, Ghats of Varanasi, Varanasi, India - Close to ghats
5.7/10 · 38 reviews

Featuring currency exchange and a lift, the comfortable Hotel Augusto Varanasi is nearly 25 minutes' walk from the Assi Ghat. Situated within reach of the bustling Dashashwamedh Ghat Riverside Steps, the 3-star hotel has 2 onsite restaurants serving Chinese, Indian, and vegetarian cuisine.

Location

This Varanasi hotel is nestled in the Ghats of Varanasi district of Varanasi, in close proximity to the cafeteria Cafe D Benaras. Attractions, you can find 2.9 km from the Augusto Varanasi, include the famous Sankat Mochan Hanuman Temple. The Grand Tulsi Manas Hindu Temple is about 30 minutes on foot from the accommodation. The hotel provides a place to stay within a 3-km distance of the Varanasi Junction train station.

Rooms

There are 26 rooms at the Augusto, each of them has an ironing set and climate control, as well as entertainment amenities such as a flat-screen TV with satellite channels. There is also a private bathroom.

Food & Drinks

This accommodation has the Indian restaurant where guests can sample examples of Chinese cuisine.
