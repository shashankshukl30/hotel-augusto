## Bangali Tola

Stretching along the Ganges River, Bangali Tola is home to a number of ghats, steps sacred to Hindu pilgrims and used in religious bathing. Wooden boats bob below Dashashwamedh Ghat, site of nightly fire rituals with chanting and floating diya oil lamps, while Man Mandir Ghat is topped by the 1700s Man Singh Observatory. The waterfront is lined with temples, and nearby alleys are full of homey vegetarian cafes.
