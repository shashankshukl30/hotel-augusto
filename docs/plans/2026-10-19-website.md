# Hotel Augusto Website Implementation Plan

**Goal:** Deliver a distinctive hotel website for Hotel Augusto, Varanasi, with Lando-led composition, Apple/Linear control clarity, a complete 17-page hospitality journey, persistent enquiry controls and a clear path to a future booking engine.

**Architecture:** Next.js App Router server components own factual content and route metadata. Small client components own menu, motion, galleries, room comparison, arrival tabs, the universal booking dock and stay/group/dining enquiries. FAQs use native details elements. Typed content modules supply hotel facts, room photographs, practical guidance and dated source-linked reviews. Static export uses a local responsive-image manifest; normal Next operation retains its image optimizer.

**Tech stack:** Next.js 16.4, strict TypeScript, Tailwind v4, Framer Motion, lucide-react, local font assets, Playwright; Vercel-ready in sin1.

## Brief and assumptions
- Premium execution for a real hotel client; truthful positioning, not unsupported luxury claims.
- Visitors include couples, families, and pilgrims planning a Varanasi stay.
- English website, mobile first. No rates or inventory until a booking engine is supplied.
- WhatsApp is an enquiry channel, not a confirmed reservation. Do not send messages automatically.
- Static page content and Next.js image optimization keep operation straightforward; no stored enquiry data, payment processing, authentication, analytics or database at this stage.
- Digiocular maintains the source; a private independent Git repository is required. No production domain changes are part of this task.
- The Google-listed phone, +91 80817 09146, is the current WhatsApp destination. Its public WhatsApp landing page resolves; the hotel must confirm the active booking account and number ownership before public launch.

## Art direction and decisions
- Latest user steering on 20 October names `landonorris.com` as the strongest visual reference, with major inspiration also requested from `linear.app`. This supersedes an Apple-only interpretation: Lando leads composition and memorable motion; Apple/Linear inform precise controls and hierarchy. Vagmi, Shree Ganesha Palace, Casa Angelina and Awwwards inform hospitality features and photographic pacing. Earlier serif/bronze decisions remain superseded.
- Use one local Inter family with system-sans fallbacks. Shared OKLCH tokens use near-white, soft cool gray, charcoal and cobalt blue. Semibold headings use tight tracking, with main interior titles around 64–80px desktop / 48px mobile and section headings around 64px / 40px. Body text stays readable at roughly 18px desktop / 17px mobile. Use rounded 24px photographs, restrained panels, 52px pill actions and 44px minimum interactive targets. Mobile inputs remain at least 16px.
- Lead with nearly full-viewport genuine Augusto guest-room, lounge and restaurant photography. Place the large left-aligned “Your place / in Kashi.” headline over the photograph, with three scene controls and pause inside it. Wait for each image to be ready before switching scenes. Spring-driven pointer drift and native scroll scaling provide motion; hover and keyboard focus pause independently. Keep the date, party and room enquiry bar in its own clear section.
- Give the city itinerary its own ink-blue chapter, oversized decorative KASHI lettering behind genuine river imagery and manual tabs. Destination photographs stay explicitly labelled in city content. Preserve reduced-motion alternatives and native scrolling throughout.
- Use the supplied `logo.jpeg` and public brand copy in the header, footer, favicon, OG image and guest planning guide. Do not replace or reinterpret the artwork.
- Preserve the full hospitality journey: welcome, essentials, all three room categories, comparison, dining, four-stage city itinerary, an exact attributed guest excerpt and review links, gallery, arrival planning, practical FAQs, stay guides and a final dated enquiry. Do not import Vagmi-specific rates, discounts, amenities, restaurant branding or reviews.
- Deliver all 17 content pages, including `/group-stays`, `/rooms/compare`, `/guides` and three practical guide articles. Room pages keep multi-image walkthroughs, enlarged keyboard/swipe viewers, factual bed arrangements, context-aware enquiries and other-room navigation.
- Keep check-in/check-out and “Check availability” accessible in a universal persistent dock. Its measured height and safe-area spacing reserve bottom space through the footer. Stay enquiries include 1–8 requested rooms and calculated night summaries. These are requests, not capacity or inventory claims.
- `/group-stays` handles family, friends and business accommodation requests. `/dining` has a specific enquiry form and one-page PDF planning guide with an editable DOCX twin. No event venue, banquet, wedding, fixed menu, price or serving-hour claims are supported.
- Show the source-specific Google snapshot, 3.7/5 from 1,628 reviews, checked 20 October 2026, with a profile link. Keep MMT as a review link because indexed scores disagree. Preserve the existing exact Tripadvisor excerpt. Do not add guest portraits without verified reuse permission.
- Temple and ghat proximity is expressed as walking distance with Google walking-direction links. Do not convert conflicting estimates into fixed times or distances.
- Mobile gets separate photograph crops, a full-height native navigation dialog, full-width narrow form fields and a two-room comparison selector. Validated dates, party details, room count and room preference travel between pages; names and personal notes stay out of the page URL.
- Motion includes calm typography entrances, photo crossfades, room-photo hover/focus feedback, menu transitions, manual city transitions and once-only reveals. Reduced motion disables displacement and autoplay. SSR content stays readable; native scrolling is never hijacked.
- No simulated availability, fabricated ratings, stock hotel rooms, artificial video, heavy WebGL, loading gates or scroll hijacking.
- Apply the latest reference hierarchy without transferring anyone else's identity: Lando for composition, Apple/Linear for control precision, and hotel references for substantive guest journeys. No reference imagery, proprietary fonts, racing/SaaS content or marketing claims are reused. Preserve the Google/MMT investigation, source-to-page mapping and genuine-photo provenance.
- Generate responsive WebP variants for static delivery without changing or upscaling originals. Keep source hashes, provenance and runtime manifest aligned. Preserve Tailwind's `source(none)` plus explicit app/components/content scan paths; the old broad scan encountered a transient Word file during development.
