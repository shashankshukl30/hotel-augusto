# Hotel Augusto Website Implementation Plan

**Goal:** Build an original, carefully art-directed hotel website for Hotel Augusto, Varanasi, with WhatsApp enquiries and a clear path to a future booking engine.

**Architecture:** Next.js App Router server components own factual content and route metadata. Small client components own menu, motion, gallery, room selection and booking enquiry interaction. FAQs use native details elements. One typed content module supplies hotel contact and room data.

**Tech stack:** Next.js 16.4, strict TypeScript, Tailwind v4, Framer Motion, lucide-react, local font assets, Playwright; Vercel-ready in sin1.

## Brief and assumptions
- Premium execution for a real hotel client; truthful positioning, not unsupported luxury claims.
- Visitors include couples, families, and pilgrims planning a Varanasi stay.
- English website, mobile first. No rates or inventory until a booking engine is supplied.
- WhatsApp is an enquiry channel, not a confirmed reservation. Do not send messages automatically.
- Static page content and Next.js image optimization keep operation straightforward; no stored enquiry data, payment processing, authentication, analytics or database at this stage.
- Digiocular maintains the source; a private independent Git repository is required. No production domain changes are part of this task.
- The Google-listed phone, +91 80817 09146, is the current WhatsApp destination. Its public WhatsApp landing page resolves; the hotel must confirm the active booking account and number ownership before public launch.

## Art direction and decisions
- Latest user correction on 20 October: “most elements what Hotel Vagmi has, but in Augusto style and UI/UX.” This supersedes the earlier product-like Apple layout. Keep clarity and substantial controls while building the complete hospitality journey. Live Vagmi, Shree Ganesha Palace and Awwwards research informs hierarchy and composition; evidence and the precise feature map live in `research/hospitality-revision/`.
- Use local Bodoni Moda400, including selective italics, for display and Manrope for body. Shared OKLCH tokens use warm ivory, deep ink blue and bronze. Asymmetric room spreads, restrained corners, fine rules and alternating light/dark sections create a hotel identity. Hero display scales to136px, primary controls are54px, inputs are16px or larger on mobile.
- Lead with genuine Augusto guest-room, lounge and restaurant photographs. Destination photographs stay explicitly labelled in city content. The hero has three scenes, manual scene controls, a pause button and bounded native-scroll movement. A date, party and room enquiry bar overlaps its lower edge.
- Home follows the hospitality journey: welcome, essentials, all three room categories, comparison, dining, four-stage city itinerary, independent review source links, gallery, arrival planning, practical FAQs, stay guides and a final dated enquiry. Do not import Vagmi-specific rates, discounts, amenities, restaurant branding or reviews.
- Expand to16contentpages: the original11 plus `/rooms/compare`, `/guides` and three useful guide articles. Room pages include multi-image walkthroughs, enlarged keyboard/swipe viewers, factual bed arrangements, context-aware date enquiry forms and other-room navigation.
- Mobile gets separate photograph crops, a full-height native navigation dialog, full-width narrow form fields and a two-room comparison selector. The enquiry form preserves dates and party details between pages, then prepares a WhatsApp draft. Names and personal notes never go in the page URL.
- Motion includes subtle hero typography entrances and photo crossfades, bounded photograph parallax, manual city transitions and once-only reveals. Reduced motion disables displacement and autoplay. SSR content stays readable; native scrolling is never hijacked.
- No simulated availability, fabricated ratings, stock hotel rooms, artificial video, heavy WebGL, loading gates or scroll hijacking.
- Live Awwwards hospitality references inform composition and page hierarchy. Apple principles inform clarity. No reference imagery, proprietary fonts or copy is reused. Revisit the exact user-supplied MakeMyTrip listing and Google profile, preserving source-to-page and photo provenance.
